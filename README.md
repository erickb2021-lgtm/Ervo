## How to use Ervo

1. Choose **Personal** (for your own life) or **Business** (for your company's customers), then sign in with your number or email.
2. Text Ervo what you need in your own words, like "dinner for 4 in two weeks at 7", or tap the **mic** and say it. Ervo works out what you mean first, asks about anything missing, then checks with you before it does anything.
3. When Ervo needs another app, like Google Calendar or Amazon, it asks first. Tap **Allow** to open it (or its sign-in page), or **Deny**.
4. Open the menu (**⋮**) for:
   - **Ervo Stats**: your steps (Android), what's coming up, and everything Ervo has done for you.
   - **Connected apps**: turn each app on or off.
   - **Greeting**: your nickname, plus the time, date, weather and today's news when you open Ervo.
5. Tap **+** to send a photo or a link.

This is an early preview: sign-in codes aren't sent yet, and some answers are samples. To turn on Ervo's full AI and see what each sync needs, read [server/README.md](server/README.md).

## Why I built Ervo

I wanted an AI helper that actually gets things done, not just one that answers questions. Ervo is built to take care of the small tasks that eat up your day, like planning, booking and returns, and to help businesses answer their customers quickly and fairly.

Just as important, Ervo keeps you in charge. It asks before it buys anything or shares your information, and the personal and business versions are kept completely separate.
