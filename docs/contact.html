Founder and CEO of Ervo

Erick S. Bardales

Erick leads Ervo with one goal: an AI helper that businesses and everyday people can trust with real work, and that gives them back their time.

He holds a degree in Computer Science and works in AI, where he helps make AI systems more accurate and reliable. He founded Ervo in 2026 after seeing how much of a day goes to small tasks: the appointment that needs moving, the return that needs starting, the customer still waiting on a reply. Ervo handles that work while you stay in charge of every decision.

His vision is an AI you can hand real work to: one that does it quietly, asks before anything that matters, and serves a company as well as it serves a busy life.
