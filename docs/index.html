Pick your Ervo

Two separate apps with separate jobs. Each one only does what it's built for.

Personal use only

Ervo Personal

Your own AI for everyday life.

  • Shops, tracks orders and handles your returns
  • Plans your week, trips and reservations
  • Builds workout and meal plans
  • Sorts your inbox and reminds you of bills
  • Won't answer customers or touch business data
Get Ervo Personal
Business use only

Ervo for Business

An AI support agent for your customers.

  • Answers customer tickets by chat, email and phone
  • Grants refunds and exchanges within your policy
  • Learns your help docs, products and rules
  • Hands tricky cases to your team with a summary
  • Won't shop, run errands or do anything personal
Get Ervo for Business
Both can 📅 Manage calendars 📞 Make phone calls 🎥 Join video meetings ✉️ Write emails

Checks your refund policy, approves it and updates your records.
