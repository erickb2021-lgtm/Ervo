Pick your Ervo

Two separate apps with separate jobs. Each one only does what it's built for.

Ervo Personal

Your own AI for everyday life. Personal use only.

  • Finds products and handles your returns, and asks before you buy
  • Adds, moves and renames calendar events, with Undo
  • Sets alarms and timers, and calls people in your contacts
  • "Hey Ervo": just say its name, and it listens
  • Psyche: remembers what you like, only for you
  • Won't answer customers or touch business data
Get Ervo Personal

Ervo for Business

An AI support agent for your customers. Business use only.

  • Answers customers and grants refunds within your policy
  • Your team joins with employee IDs; trusted people become co-owners
  • Business Ideas: billing, taxes, suppliers and renewals
  • Business Psyche: learns your products, policies and customers
  • Hands tricky cases to your team with a summary
  • Won't shop, run errands or do anything personal
Get Ervo for Business
